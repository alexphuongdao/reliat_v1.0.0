# Continuous crushing-circuit background film

## Primary prompt

Create a photorealistic industrial mining process film showing one physically
coherent iron-ore crushing and screening circuit in continuous operation. Use a
single uninterrupted wide shot from a high three-quarter observation-platform
view, with either a locked camera or an extremely slow, stable lateral dolly.
The entire material path must remain spatially understandable within one real
mine processing area: large, irregular run-of-mine iron ore enters a steel feed
hopper; a heavy-duty primary crusher visibly reduces it into smaller angular
fragments; the crushed ore transfers naturally onto rugged conveying equipment;
the conveyor feeds a real inclined vibrating screen; the screen visibly
separates coarse and fine particle-size fractions onto different discharge
paths; and the final product stream leaving the circuit is clearly more
controlled and uniform in size than the incoming feed.

All stages operate simultaneously as one continuous steady-state process, so
the result can loop without a narrative reset. Preserve strict material
continuity: the same dark red-brown hematite and magnetite ore must move through
every stage at believable speed and volume. Show correct gravity, realistic
rock collisions, belt loading, crusher motion, screen vibration, transfer-point
dust, worn steel, abrasion, grease, guard rails, motors, chutes, idlers, and
full-scale mine infrastructure. Dust should be subtle and localized, catching
natural overcast daylight without hiding the machinery or material flow. The
image should feel like premium documentary footage commissioned by a major
mining operator: restrained, credible, technically grounded, cinematic but not
stylized, with natural slate, charcoal, oxidized iron, muted earth, and dark
industrial-green tones.

Compose for a website background. Keep the main crusher-to-screen process in
the central and right portions of the frame, preserve some darker visual
breathing room on the left for white headline text, and keep critical machinery
inside the center-safe area for responsive crops. Hold exposure, white balance,
camera position, material flow rate, and lighting constant. End on motion and
composition that can crossfade invisibly back to the opening frames. No text,
logos, people in the foreground, graphics, labels, or interface overlays.

## Recommended generation settings

- Duration: 12–16 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape
- Resolution: highest available; deliver 3840×2160 or at least 1920×1080
- Frame rate: 24 or 30 fps, constant frame rate
- Motion: low-to-moderate, physically realistic, no camera cut
- Camera: locked or very slow stabilized dolly
- Loop: seamless or crossfade-safe, with continuous material already moving in
  both the opening and closing frames
- Audio: none required
- Web master: H.264 MP4, `yuv420p`, fast-start enabled, no audio track

Generate the 16:9 master first. If the tool supports reference-based variants,
derive a separate 9:16 version from the approved master while preserving the
same plant geometry, material color, machinery, timing, and lighting; do not ask
the model to invent a second circuit independently.
